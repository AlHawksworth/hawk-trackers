📚 Help & Tutorial

🚀 Getting Started

  • Import existing visits using the 📥 Import button
  • Browse stadiums and add them to your 🎯 Bucket List
  • Use ⚡ Quick Rate for fast visit entry
  • Toggle between 🌙 Dark and ☀️ Light themes

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

1-5 Navigate pages
Ctrl+Q Quick rate
Ctrl+T Toggle theme
Esc Close modals
←→ Navigate content

🏆 Achievements

Unlock badges by completing milestones like visiting your first stadium, exploring different tiers, or building visit streaks. Check the Social page to see your progress!

📱 Mobile Tips

  • Install as a PWA for offline access
  • Enable location services for nearby stadiums
  • Use Quick Rate during matches for fast entry
  • Upload photos directly from your camera
