# 🏟️ Hawk Football Travels

A comprehensive groundhopping platform for rating, reviewing, and tracking football stadium visits across English leagues and beyond.

## ✨ Features

### Core Functionality
- **Stadium Database**: 200+ stadiums from Premier League to Step 6
- **Comprehensive Rating System**: 10-category scoring with visual radar charts
- **Visit Tracking**: Import/export visit data with detailed reviews
- **Bucket List**: Mark stadiums you want to visit
- **Progress Tracking**: Visual progress through league tiers

### 🆕 Enhanced Features

#### **Social & Community**
- **Achievement System**: Unlock badges for milestones and challenges
- **Profile Sharing**: Share your groundhopping journey with others
- **Visit Streaks**: Track consecutive visit patterns
- **Leaderboards**: Compare progress with friends (coming soon)

#### **Smart Discovery & Planning**
- **Interactive Map**: Visual stadium locations with visit status
- **Nearby Stadiums**: GPS-based discovery of local grounds
- **Distance Sorting**: Find stadiums by proximity
- **Price Tracking**: Sort by ticket costs and spending analysis

#### **Enhanced Data & Analytics**
- **Advanced Filtering**: By region, tier, distance, price, visit status
- **Detailed Dashboard**: Statistics, trends, and insights
- **Export Options**: PDF reports and CSV data export
- **Photo Upload**: Add stadium photos to your visits
- **Voice Notes**: Record audio reviews (coming soon)

#### **User Experience**
- **Dark/Light Mode**: Toggle between themes with keyboard shortcut (Ctrl+T)
- **Quick Rate**: Fast visit entry for on-the-go rating
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Power user navigation (1-5 for pages, Ctrl+Q for quick rate)
- **Offline Mode**: Cache data for offline browsing
- **PWA Support**: Install as mobile app

## 🎮 Usage

### Getting Started
1. Open the app in your browser
2. Use the **Import** button to load existing visit data
3. Browse stadiums by league tier, region, or search
4. Click any stadium to view details or add to bucket list

### Quick Actions
- **⚡ Quick Rate**: Rapid visit entry during/after matches
- **📷 Photos**: Upload stadium images to your visits
- **📤 Share**: Export your profile or individual reviews
- **🎯 Bucket List**: Plan future groundhopping adventures

### Keyboard Shortcuts
- `1-5`: Navigate between pages
- `Ctrl+Q`: Open quick rate modal
- `Ctrl+T`: Toggle light/dark theme
- `Esc`: Close modals
- `←/→`: Navigate modal content

## 📊 Rating System

Each visit is scored across 10 categories (1-10 points each):

1. **👀 Sightlines & View** - Visibility and pitch proximity
2. **🏛️ Architecture & Character** - Historic stands and unique features
3. **🌧️ Cover & Pitch** - Weather protection and pitch quality
4. **👫 Happy Wife, Happy Life** - Local area safety and appeal
5. **🍺 In-Ground Bars & Concourses** - Facilities and refreshments
6. **🍻 Pre-Match Local Scene** - Nearby pubs and atmosphere
7. **📢 Atmosphere & Fan Noise** - Match day experience
8. **🤝 Club Welcome & Hospitality** - Staff friendliness and programs
9. **🚆 Travel & Transit** - Transport links and accessibility
10. **🎫 Ticket Value** - Price relative to experience quality

## 🏆 Achievement System

Unlock badges by completing challenges:

- **🏟️ First Step**: Visit your first stadium
- **⭐ Getting Started**: Visit 5 different stadiums
- **🔥 Regular**: Visit 10 different stadiums
- **🏙️ London Explorer**: Visit 5 London stadiums
- **💯 Perfectionist**: Give a stadium 100% rating
- **🎯 Tier Traveller**: Visit stadiums from 4 different tiers
- **📅 Monthly Visitor**: Visit 3 stadiums in one month
- **💰 Big Spender**: Spend £100+ on tickets
- **🚗 Distance Traveller**: Visit stadiums in 3+ regions
- **✍️ Detailed Reviewer**: Write 5 detailed hawk tips
